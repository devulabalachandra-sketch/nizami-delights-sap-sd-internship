# Nizami Delights – SAP SD Internship Project

## Project Overview

This repository contains the project work being carried out as part of the **Junior SAP SD Consultant Internship**.

The project uses **Nizami Delights** as a simulated business organization to understand and document SAP Sales and Distribution processes in an SAP S/4HANA environment.

The project focuses on business requirements gathering, process mapping, master data analysis, sales order processing, delivery, billing, and related SAP SD business processes.

## Internship Details

- **Role:** Junior SAP SD Consultant Intern
- **SAP Module:** Sales and Distribution (SD)
- **SAP Platform:** SAP S/4HANA
- **Project:** Nizami Delights – SAP SD Implementation
- **Project Type:** Functional / Business Process Implementation
- **Current Phase:** Week 1 – Business Requirements and Process Mapping
- **Status:** Ongoing

## Business Scenario

Nizami Delights is a simulated food and confectionery organization that sells sweets, bakery products, snacks, and packaged food products to retail and wholesale customers.

The SAP SD solution is being studied and developed to support the organization's sales and distribution activities, including customer management, product sales, pricing, sales order processing, availability checking, outbound delivery, goods issue, and billing.

## Week 1 – Business Requirements and Process Mapping

The first phase of the internship focuses on the initial SAP SD implementation activities.

### Key Activities

- Understanding business requirements
- Identifying stakeholders and business processes
- Understanding SAP SD organizational structure
- Identifying master data requirements
- Mapping the end-to-end sales process
- Defining sales order requirements
- Defining delivery requirements
- Defining billing requirements
- Identifying integration points with MM and FI
- Documenting assumptions and potential challenges
- Recommending SAP SD implementation strategies and best practices

## End-to-End Sales Process

The planned sales process is:

**Customer Inquiry → Quotation → Sales Order → Availability Check → Outbound Delivery → Picking & Packing → Post Goods Issue → Billing → Accounting**

Inquiry and quotation may be optional depending on the business requirement.

## Master Data

The project considers the following major master-data requirements:

- Business Partner / Customer
- Material Master
- Customer-Material Information
- Pricing Conditions
- Output / Communication Data

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop practical understanding of how a junior SAP SD consultant gathers business requirements, maps business processes, identifies master data, and prepares a structured foundation for SAP SD implementation.
